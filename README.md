  ## What it does

  Clipper lives in your MacBook's notch. It stays hidden until you need it, hover to peek, click to expand. No menu bar clutter, no floating windows.

  **Now Playing**
  See what's playing from any app, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music. Album art, live scrubber, shuffle, and transport controls. The UI tints itself to match
  the artwork.

  **Clipboard Board** *(coming soon)*
  Every copy, instantly accessible. Paste anything from your history without breaking your flow.

  **Timer & Notifications** *(coming soon)*
  Live activities and smart alerts, right in the notch.

  ---

  ## Features

  - Smooth notch → island expand animation
  - Now playing with artwork glow, marquee titles, and music visualizer
  - Artwork-tinted progress bar and controls
  - App icon badge — know which player is active
  - Hover to peek · Click to pin · Esc to dismiss

  ## Built with

  `SwiftUI` `AppKit` `MediaRemote` `Combine`

  ## Requirements
